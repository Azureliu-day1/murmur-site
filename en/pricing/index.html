Questions

How is the allowance counted?

On the free tier, cloud dictation is counted in characters: 2,000 a week. Long recording is counted by recording time: 30 minutes a month, with a seat. On-device recognition is not counted.

Does a recording stop when I run out?

No. The recording you are in finishes and is saved as usual, and the app tells you the allowance is used up. Cloud allowance stops from the next recording.

How do I cancel?

Write to us at support@day1aifitness.com and we will cancel it for you.

Cancel any time on your Stripe account page.

Is payment safe?
