How it works

Here is what it looks like with the watch. Recording on the iPhone is simpler still, since you start and stop it right there.

  1. 1

    Tap the orange button

    If you are sitting at the back of a talk and would rather not dig out your phone, lift your wrist and tap once.

  2. 10:09

    2

    Recording with the screen off

    Put your arm down and the screen dims, but the recording carries on. It is saved on the watch first, so your phone does not have to be with you.

  3. 3

    Press and hold to stop

    When you are done, keep a finger on the screen until it says Saved, then let go.

  4. Sending to iPhone

    4

    Get near your iPhone

    Once the watch is close to your iPhone, it sends the recording over by itself. You do not need to do anything.

  5. 38:12

    Speaker 1: Why open your own shop?

    Speaker 2: I didn't plan it at first.

    5

    Open the transcript

    The phone transcribes what it receives and puts the finished text in History. Tap it to read.

Things people ask

How long can it record?

Your iPhone can record an hour-long meeting. Leave it on the table and you never have to check on it.

Can it tell who is talking?

Yes, in the full transcript you get after recording. It is split into Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on, which helps most with interviews. The text you see while recording is only a preview and is not split yet.

What if transcription fails?

The recording is kept. A failed transcription does not take the audio with it.

Where are recordings stored?

Your recordings and history are kept on your own device.
