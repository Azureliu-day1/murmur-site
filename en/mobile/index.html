Apple Watch

Raise your wrist, tap

01
Tap to startTap once on the watch to start. It keeps recording with the screen off.
02
Hold to stopWhen you are done, press and hold to stop.
03
Sent to your phoneThe recording is saved on the watch first, then sent to your phone when it is nearby. Your phone transcribes it.

Sign in on your phone and the watch signs in too, with the same account.
