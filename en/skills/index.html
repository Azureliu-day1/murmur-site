Prompt skills

You give the gist. The AI gets a complete request.

In the AI coding and chat tools you already use, what you say is first turned into one clear passage. When Murmur can tell what kind of task it is, it adds a few steps on how to approach it at the end, so the AI takes fewer wrong turns and you spend less time explaining.
